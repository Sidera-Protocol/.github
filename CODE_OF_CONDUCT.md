# Code of Conduct

This applies to every Sidera Protocol repository, and to issues, pull requests,
reviews and discussions on them.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Sidera to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect people's time: read the issue and `CONTRIBUTING.md` before asking, and
  wait to be assigned before starting on an issue.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to anyone they affected, and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or sexualised language or imagery
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, and deliberately derailing discussion
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Submitting code you know to be harmful, such as malware or hidden backdoors
- Spam, including low-effort pull requests opened only to claim an issue

## Reporting

Contact a maintainer privately on GitHub ([@Meet-hybrid](https://github.com/Meet-hybrid)).
For security vulnerabilities, use private vulnerability reporting on the affected
repository instead (see its `SECURITY.md`).

Every report is read, kept as confidential as possible, and answered.

## What happens next

Maintainers decide on a response that fits what happened. That can be a private
warning, removing comments or contributions, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban
from the organization's repositories.
